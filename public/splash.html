Why Venus?

Venus Protocol is the leading algorithmic money market on BNB Smart Chain. It is where DiamondWall's Treasury deposits every BNB collected in presale to generate real, verifiable yield.

When the Treasury deposits BNB in Venus, it receives vBNB tokens in return. These vBNB tokens accrue value every block as Venus pays out interest from real borrowing activity. When DiamondWall needs BNB back (to pay rewards, liquidity, or referrals), the Treasury simply redeems vBNB for BNB plus accumulated yield.

  • Battle-tested since 2020 with billions processed
  • Multi-audited by major security firms
  • Real yield from actual borrowing demand — not token emissions
  • Fully transparent — Treasury's vBNB balance is verifiable on-chain
  • Instant liquidity: DiamondWall can redeem BNB at any moment
